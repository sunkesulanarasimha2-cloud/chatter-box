Our Story

A kitchen built on memory

Spice & Soul began in 2011 as a twelve-table dining room inspired by the Sunday kitchens of Chef Arjun's grandmother in Kerala. Today it remains a family-run restaurant where every masala is ground in-house and every plate is finished by hand.

Fresh IngredientsVegetables and herbs from partner farms, seafood landed each morning, whole spices roasted daily.
Traditional RecipesHeirloom techniques: dum-sealed biryanis, clay-oven breads and gravies cooked low for hours.
Modern PresentationContemporary plating and a considered wine, mocktail and chai pairing for every course.
